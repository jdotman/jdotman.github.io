The bench shows no sign the work was interrupted, every tool laid out with the same care a careful craftsman gives to any finished piece. You lift one lens and hold it to the torchlight, surprised by how clearly it still focuses the flame.
Cracked hourglasses line the shelves of the timekeeper's workshop, sand settled permanently into the lower half of every one despite whatever mechanism once existed to turn them back over. Every desk still sits arranged as though the next turning were only moments away, though no hand has come to manage it. You lift the nearest glass, watching a single grain finally shift after untold centuries of stillness.
Splintered handles and rusted heads fill the toolmaker's bin in the abandoned smithy annex, every piece separated from whatever whole it once belonged to by damage no one stayed long enough to repair. Theft seems unlikely here, every remaining piece simply too broken for anyone to have bothered carrying away. You sort through the pile carefully, unsure whether any of it could still be made useful.
